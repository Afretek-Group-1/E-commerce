🔄 30-Day Free Returns & Size Exchanges

We understand that purchasing shoes and apparel online requires accurate sizing. Mercado offers a free 30-day exchange window for any incorrect sizes.

  • Items must be unworn, undamaged, with original tags intact.
  • Shoes must be tried on indoors on clean carpet or flooring.
  • Once returned, exchanges are dispatched within 24 hours, or refunds credited within 3-5 business days.

📦 Shipping Timelines & Rates

Deliveries are managed directly by certified local and regional couriers with live SMS tracking.

  • Free Shipping: Automatically applied to all shopping bags over $50 USD.
  • Standard Delivery: $5.00 flat rate for orders under $50 USD.
  • Local/Metro Areas: 1 to 3 business days.
  • Regional/Outlying: 3 to 6 business days.

💵 Cash on Delivery & Payment Security

To ensure 100% financial security, we support Cash on Delivery (COD) alongside secure digital payment methods (Mobile Money, Visa, Mastercard).

Customer Right: When opting for Cash on Delivery, you are entitled to inspect the sealed outer parcel in front of the courier to verify package integrity before handing over payment.
